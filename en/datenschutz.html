Privacy policy

Principle

We take the protection of your personal data seriously. We treat your personal data confidentially and in accordance with the statutory data protection regulations and this privacy policy.

Data collection on this website

This website does not use cookies or tracking technologies. We do not collect any personal data unless you voluntarily send it to us by e-mail.

Feedback by e-mail

The feedback form does not store anything on this website; it prepares a message to us in your e-mail program. If you write to us, your details, including your contact information, will be stored by us to process your request and in case of follow-up questions.

Maps

For the directions map we embed a map section from OpenStreetMap. When the map is displayed, your IP address is transmitted to the OpenStreetMap servers.

SSL/TLS encryption

For security reasons and to protect the transmission of confidential content, this website uses SSL/TLS encryption.

Your rights

You have the right to information about the data stored about you. If you have questions about the collection, processing or use of your personal data, please contact us.
