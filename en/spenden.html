Donate

Café International is a place to meet – open to everyone and free of charge. A team of volunteers makes this possible, and the Reformed parish hall provides the rooms.

Still, there are expenses: for coffee and cake, small decorations, gifts for guest speakers and training for our volunteers. Most of this is covered by the City of Adliswil, the Catholic Church Adliswil and the Reformed Church in the Sihltal.

With your donation you help keep Café International a place to meet in Adliswil – and allow us to expand what we offer. Every amount helps: via your banking app, the donation box on site or as a collection in your parish.

Thank you very much!
