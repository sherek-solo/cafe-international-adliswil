Welcome to Café International

At Café International in Adliswil, people from here and from all over the world meet.

Over a cup of coffee or tea, conversations start easily: about world events and the small worries of everyday life, about life in Switzerland and in our guests’ home countries.

A special welcome goes to everyone who is new to Adliswil, speaks another language or spends a lot of time alone or at home with small children. Here you will find contact, exchange and a listening ear.

Everything is free of charge, and there is a play corner for children. We look forward to seeing you!
