History

The idea (2016)

On 2 March 2016, the church social workers Silvia Bänziger (Reformed) and Daniela Kaiser (Catholic) met with other initiators in the Reformed parish hall. Their idea: a café where people could meet in Adliswil. After several meetings the concept was ready, and the church councils of both churches gave their approval.

The aim

The café was to be a place for everyone in Adliswil, especially for people with a migration background and for the residents of the emergency shelter at that time. It was meant to bring locals and newcomers together, be easy to access and offer a chance to practise German. It was supported by the Catholic Church Adliswil, the Reformed parish of Adliswil and the Reformed Church of Langnau a. A.

The start

The Reformed parish hall was the obvious choice: central, well equipped, with a park in summer and no extra room costs. To fit in well with the “Treff+” of the former Chrischona congregation and the “Brotchorb” of the Methodist Church, the “Begegnungskafi” opened on the 1st and 3rd Friday of the month from 9 to 11 am. The first opening was on 4 November 2016.

Today

Since then, the day and times have changed, and the programme and public presence have developed further. The basic idea has remained the same: a place where everyone in Adliswil can meet – in other words: finding connection, feeling at home. In 2026, Café International celebrated its tenth anniversary with an open day on 12 September.
